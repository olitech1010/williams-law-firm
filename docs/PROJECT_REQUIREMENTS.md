# PROJECT_REQUIREMENTS: The Williams Law Firm

## 1. Project Overview
**Product:** Law Firm Website (Static/Brochure)
**Client:** The Williams Law Firm
**Principal Attorney:** Randall DeWitt Williams, Esq.
**Tech Stack:** Next.js (No database or backend connection).
**Primary Goal:** Establish a professional, community-focused digital presence for a solo practitioner law firm in Edgefield, SC, highlighting expertise in Workers' Compensation, Personal Injury, Probate & Estate Administration, and General Litigation.

## 2. Target Audience
- Individuals, workers, and families across Edgefield County and the Central Savannah River Area (CSRA).
- People seeking representation for workplace injuries, car accidents, or civil disputes.
- Families needing assistance with probate and estate administration.

## 3. Core Features & Workflows
1. **Homepage / Hero:** 
   - Full-width hero section with a Call-to-Action button linking to call (803) 637-4902.
   - Headline: "Experienced Legal Guidance for Edgefield and the CSRA".
   - Imagery: Classic timber desk, law books, and courthouse pillars.
2. **Trust Badges:**
   - 3-Column Highlights: 30+ Years Licensed • Licensed in SC & NC • Local Community Focus.
3. **Practice Areas:**
   - 4-Card Grid covering: Workers' Comp, Personal Injury, Probate, Civil Litigation.
4. **About the Attorney:**
   - Split Two-Column bio featuring Randall DeWitt Williams' academic background and 30+ years of service.
5. **Intake / Contact:**
   - Client intake form (Name, Phone, Legal Issue).
   - Direct Google Map embed targeting 201 Buncombe St, Edgefield, SC 29824.
   - Mailing address details.

## 4. UI/UX & Design Guidelines
- **Theme:** Authentic, trustworthy, and community-focused.
- **Imagery Strategy:** Since there is no existing branded digital photography, use high-resolution, uncopyrighted legal and regional assets (Unsplash/Pexels).
- **Assets:** Custom professional logo for "The Williams Law Firm".

## 5. Technical Constraints
- **Framework:** Next.js.
- **Backend/DB:** STRICTLY NONE. All content is hardcoded or loaded from local files.
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS.

## 6. Execution Plan
1. **Asset Generation:** Create the custom logo and gather professional imagery.
2. **Component Development:** Build layout, navigation, footer, hero, and content cards.
3. **Content Population:** Migrate all text from `about.md` into the respective pages and components.
